# Target research, 13 October 2026

US$60,000 a year is the list-price scenario for 25 Salesforce Revenue Cloud Advanced users at US$200 per user per month, billed annually. Sales Cloud is additional. This is not a customer invoice or a claim that every small business pays that amount.

[Salesforce pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/revenue-lifecycle-management/pricing/), opened this run, lists Growth at US$150 and Advanced at US$200 per user per month billed annually. The official price page still uses Revenue Cloud. We use that name without making a product retirement claim.

[eSpatial pricing](https://www.espatial.com/pricing/euro) lists US$4,999 per user per year. Ten users imply US$49,990. Its map and route experience is central, so it fails the useful-without-a-front-end test. [Reftab pricing](https://www.reftab.com/pricing) quotes larger configurations privately. A qualifying five-figure bill was not established this run, so it fails the first filter. [Alchemer small-team pricing](https://www.alchemer.com/plans-pricing/) caps Full Access at three users at US$1,895 per user per year. That tier also fails the annual-bill filter.

Buyer fit is scored conservatively. The Command Centre registry contains a published Salesforce CRM rebuild and Sam's enterprise-software bill thesis. No customer system was queried and no specific Revenue Cloud customer invoice was found. This is contextual fit, not verified product adoption.

Searches for Revenue Cloud alternatives and pricing returned comparison pages and operator discussions about configuration effort. Search demand is directional, not measured keyword volume. No complaint is repeated as a verified fact. CPQ's existing enterprise reporting can answer many of our questions with configuration; we make no exclusive capability claim.

The registry had no new build queued and no configure-price-quote family. Killed generic proposal targets are not this product catalogue, bundle and approval workflow. Published contracts-for-claude-code was cloned and read as the completed quality reference.

## Import evidence

[Salesforce: Update Product prices](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=000384199&language=en_US&type=1) documents Data Loader exports of PriceBookEntry, including product, pricebook, price, activity and source IDs. Our import takes standard-object exports with those headers. Native Revenue Cloud pricing procedures and managed-package CPQ objects need mapping. The replace guide states the boundary explicitly.

## Scope

Product catalogue, required-component rules, pricebooks, quotes, discount review, revisions and local documents. No subscription proration, consumption rating, invoicing, payment collection, e-signature, identity provider or autonomous sending. Omni by Enterprise DNA scopes connections and a customer-facing quoting screen separately.
